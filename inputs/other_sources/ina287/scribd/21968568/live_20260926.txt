A. Authority to arrest aliens:

1. With Watrant pursuant to Section 236(a) of the

2. Without warrant pursuant to Section 287 of the
Immigration and Nationality Act.

B. ICE is one of few administrative agencies with
authority to issue warrants of arrest.

1, We must be able to defend our actions before the

drastic action.
