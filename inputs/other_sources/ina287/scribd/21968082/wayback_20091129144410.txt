INTRODUCTION
The Law Enforcement Support Center (LESC or Center) was developed in response to a Congressional anti-crime initiative to respond to inquiries from federal, state, and local criminal justice agencies concerning aliens under investigation or arrest for commission of an "aggravated felony" or other criminal offenses. The Congressional mandate was originally specified in the Anti-Drug Abuse Act of 1988 and has been reiterated and modified in subsequent pieces of legislation. The Immigration and Netionallty Act was
amended as follows: .
"(A) The Attorney General shall devise and implement a system -
(i) To make available, daily (on a 24 hour basis), to give federal, state and local authorities the investigative resources of the Service to determine whether individuals arrested by such authorities for aggravated felonies are etiens; and
(ii) To designate and train officers and employees of the Service within each district to serve as a liaison to federal, state and local law enforcement and correctional agencies and courts with respect to the arrest, conviction, and release of any alien charged with an aggravated felony. "1
On March 1,2003, the Immigration and Naturalization Service was restructured into the Bureaus of Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), Customs and Border Protection (CSP). and Citizenship and Immigration Services (CIS) within the Department of Homeland Security (DHS). The LESC is an ICE Headquarters Investigations component.
Special Agents from ICE respond to a variety of criminal and administrative enforcement matters within the interior of the United States. The U.S. Border Patrol, now one of the uniformed enforcement branches of U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP), enforces the immigration laws along the borders of the United States.

TECHNICAL OPERATIONS BRANCH
The LESe serves as the central point of contact for federal, state and local criminal justice agencies querying the identity and immigration status of aliens. To perform this function, the LESC:
• Receives queries via NLETS from Law Enforcement Agencies (LEAs) relating to foreign nationals who have committed or are under investigation for criminal offenses
• Searches immigration indices to locate corresponding records relating to the subject in the LEA's inquiry
• Responds via NLETS to the LEAs, providing information from immigration indices, or advising that no matching records were found
• Notifies appropriate ICE field offices that LEAs have arrested a suspected foreign national
• Maintains a database of all LEA inquiries and the LESC responses
In addition to providing real time assistance to law enforcement aqencies that are investigating, or have arrested, foreign nationals involved in criminal activity, the LESC also performs the following investigative functions:

• Special Response Unit- The LESC is the central point of contact for the Federal Bureau of Investigation National Instant Criminal Bqckground Check System (NICS) program. Every foreign-born firearm applicant is screened by the Center for immigration status before being authorized to purchase or possess a weapon. INVESTIGATIVE SERVICES BRANCH
The LESC Investigative Services Branch (ISB) has been designed to provide the same type of timely and accurate immigration data to internal ICE investigations and field operations. The ISB is divided into special units dedicated to specific enforcement missions:
• Special Case Unit - The LESC provides support to a host of on going multiagency investigative initiatives. Working in concert with ICE field units, task forces and other local, state and federal investigators the Center gathers, identifies, analyzes and responds to thousands of off-line query requests.
• Communications Center - The Comm Center serves ICE Special Agents and Deportation Officers nationwide with after-hours telephonic enforcement support via our digitally recorded duty lines confirmed by Personal Identification Numbers.

COMPUTER LINKED APPLICATION INFORMATION MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (CLAIMS)
CLAIMS is an application processing system which provides information concerning the receipt, adjudication, and notification processes for applicants and petitioners of immigration benefits, such as Permanent Residency, Employment' Authorization Documents, etc. The current CLAIMS implementation combines several systems that support these processing requirements.
DEPORTABLE ALIEN CONTROL SYSTEM (DACS)
DACS provides information on the status and disposition of deportation cases. The DACS system captures deportable alien data, tracks aliens who are arrested, detained or formally removed from the country, and produces deportation forms and reports.
