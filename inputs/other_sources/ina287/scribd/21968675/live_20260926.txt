A. Purpose of Course

As an ICE agenvlofficer you may be required to process; detain, and transport aliens
who are children or adolescents. There are policies, regulations and a negotiated
agreement that directs how ICE Officers will perform these functions with juvenile
aliens. The purpose of this course is to provide instruction in the basic and most.
prominent obligations the Bureau has when dealing with juvenile aliens. It should
be remembered that the aliens we will be discussing are very young, some are just
children,

Flores v. Reno is a class action lawsuit that was filed against the INS in 1985. The
lawsuit challenged several aspects of INS policy on the arrest, processing,
detention, and release of minors in INS custody. Although the federal court in the
Central District of California ruled in favor of the plaintiffs on two claims in the
early stages of the litigation, the United States Supreme Court ruled in favor of the
INS on other claims in 1993. Since that decision, the parties have negotiated a
settlement of the remaining issues. INS Commissioner, Doris Meissner, signed the
negotiated agreement, and the federal court has approved its terms. The settlement
agreement became effective February 24, 1997.

B. Objectives

The objectives of the course are as follows:

1. Identify Agency policy regarding juveniles.

2. Identify the “reasonable person” standard relating to juveniles.

3. Identify the correct procedure for transporting juveniles,

4. Identify the correct procedure for processing juveniles.

6. Identify the time limitations and custody requirements for the placement of
juveniles.
