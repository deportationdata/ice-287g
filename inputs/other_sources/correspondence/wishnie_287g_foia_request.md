Dear Professor Wishnie,

I'm a researcher working on the history of ICE's 287(g) program, and I'm writing
to ask about a FOIA project the Worker & Immigrant Rights Clinic ran some years
ago.

On March 8, 2007 the clinic filed a FOIA request with ICE for 287(g) Memoranda of
Understanding, and ICE produced 34 MOUs on January 17, 2008. The clinic posted
them at islandia.law.yale.edu/wirc/287g_foia.html, along with two cover letters
and a combined ZIP file.

The page itself survives in the Internet Archive, so the list of 34 jurisdictions
is still readable:

  https://web.archive.org/web/20080807125556/http://islandia.law.yale.edu/wirc/287g_foia.html

The documents do not. The Archive only crawled the /pdfs/287_g_foia/ directory
after the site came down, so every capture of the PDFs and the ZIP is a 404.

That production looks like the earliest complete, independently verified list of
287(g) agreements anywhere, and it matters for a reason beyond its age: four of
the agencies in it -- Barnstable County MA, Framingham MA, Hudson NH, and El Paso
TX -- appear in no later ICE roster. Agreements that began and ended before ICE's
surviving published tables are otherwise invisible, so for those jurisdictions the
clinic's copies may be the only record of the agreements.

Would you by any chance still have the files, either the individual PDFs or the
ZIP? If the clinic's old web files are held elsewhere in the law school, I'd be
grateful for a pointer to the right person.

If they do turn up, I'd be glad to deposit them somewhere durable and permanent,
with credit to the clinic, so this doesn't happen again.

Thank you for considering it.

Best regards,
